Credits

Built with help. Credited properly.

Oblivion v2 is my own work, but it did not come from nowhere. These are the projects and people whose tools and ideas shaped it. A lot of scripts never say where their ideas came from, so here is the honest list.

Tools inside Oblivion

Rayfield Gen2
The switch and colour picker elements used across the menu.
LuaProt
Protects the script. I chose it after seeing Novoline, a popular script, run on it with a trusted community behind it.
SoundCloud
Powers the Music tab. An amazing catalogue to build on.

Ideas that inspired a feature

Orca
Script thumbnails while loading, and the friend activity idea.
Sirius
The dock, the Players tab and the Settings layout.
AK Admin
The idea behind the server browser.
Phantom
The idea for a Music tab, and a lot of help on the Cmds tab.
Novoline
The single-page layout of this website. I liked how their site kept everything on one page and built mine the same way.

Commands

Written for Oblivion
The Cmds tab and its command system are my own. A handful of individual commands were adapted from the admin scripts that shaped the scene: Infinite Yield, CMD-X, Nameless Admin and Novoline.
Phantom and Dreaming
Phantom (@phantomvail) and Dreaming (@n.cir) helped me build the commands and tested them over and over until they worked properly.

The Chat tab

Carried over from SYNC
I first built a global chat for SYNC, my earlier project, so bringing it to Oblivion felt natural.

Performance

Gladius
@ironbrew1 is the one who recommended the macros that fixed the lag.

A note from me.

Credit matters to me. Every name on this page helped make Oblivion better, and I would rather say that openly than pretend it all appeared on its own. Thank you to every developer behind these projects, your work taught me a lot.

Oblivion still has bugs and rough edges. I know, and I am fixing them one by one. If something is broken, a credit is missing, or you just have a thought, tell me below. I read every message.
